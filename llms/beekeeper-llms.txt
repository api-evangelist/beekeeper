# LumApps

> LumApps is an AI-powered employee hub and intranet platform that connects people, tools, AI agents, and knowledge across the employee experience. It unifies internal communication, collaboration, work automation, employee learning, and frontline operations for organizations of all sizes.

LumApps serves internal communications, HR, IT, and frontline operations teams. The platform integrates with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, ServiceNow, Workday, and 50+ business apps, and includes a governed AI Agent Hub, no-code micro-apps, and a mobile-first frontline experience. Following the combination with Beekeeper, LumApps spans both desk-based knowledge workers and deskless frontline employees.

The site is published in English (default) and localized at `/fr` (French), `/de` (German), `/it` (Italian), and `/es` (Spanish). Replace the leading path segment to reach a localized version of most pages below.
